Table fields:

"submission_id" - identifier for a submission by a participant, it may contain a few "submission_item_id".
"userid" -  participant identifier.
"timestamp" - time of a submission.
"sampleid" - unique identifier for a sample site.
"skip_reason" - It can have two values:
	NA - a sample site has not been skipped;
	1 - a sample site has been skipped due to one of the following reasons: low resolution/absence of imagery in Google maps and Microsoft Bing maps/clouds;
"submission_item_id" - it is a unique identifier for each submission item and corresponds to a field size category that has been detected (one location may contain a few field sizes)
"field_size" - field size category:
	3502 -Very large fields with an area of greater than 100 ha;
	3503 - Large fields with an area between 16 ha and 100 ha;
	3504 - Medium fields with an area between 2.56 ha and 16 ha;
	3505 - Small fields with an area between 0.64 ha and 2.56 ha;
	3506 - Very small fields with an area less than 0.64 ha;
	3507 - no fields;
	NA - skipped due to low resolution/absence of imagery in Google maps and Microsoft Bing maps/clouds 

"dominant" - it consists of two values:
		t - dominant field size;
		f - not dominant field size.
"name" - field size category names such as:
	Very small (<0.64ha);      	
	Small (0.64 ha - 2.56 ha); 
	Medium (2.56 ha -16 ha);   
	Large (16 ha - 100 ha);    
	Very large (>100 ha);
	No fields.    

"x","y" - centroids ofa 400m x 400m box in lat/lon.


This table contains submissions from 106 participants who completed more than 10 classifications.To get more information, please read the paper.
